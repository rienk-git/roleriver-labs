# AI-first matching and domain contract

The MVP assesses a complete relevant CV and vacancy text, optionally supplemented by clearly distinguished user corrections, experience and structured preferences. Preserve project history, responsibilities, technologies and dates; redact identifiers and unrelated sensitive content. Vacancy input identifies provenance, full or partial completeness, publication/deadline and status where known. Source text may be multilingual; authored instructions are English. A generated structured profile is optional later, versioned and traceable; the original CV and explicit corrections remain authoritative.

One logical AI assessment request per relevant CV/vacancy version interprets hard requirements, wishes, responsibilities, ambiguity, alternatives and conjunctions; assesses contextual equivalence, transferable skills, experience and evidence; and recommends apply, review or skip. A narrow provider adapter uses versioned instructions and structured output, validated at runtime. Bounded, observable retries may repeat the request after transient or invalid responses. No separate extraction/scoring calls, ontology, hardcoded equivalence matrix or weighted semantic scoring engine are required. Multi-stage workflows, embeddings, local models or Python matching need evaluation evidence first.

A requirement records original text, kind, met/not_met/unknown/not_applicable status, source evidence or verifiable excerpts, candidate evidence references, rationale and uncertainty. An assessment contains requirements, gaps, strengths, disqualifiers, warnings, completeness, recommendation, optional indicative score and qualitative confidence. References must resolve to supplied sources; schema validation alone cannot establish factual correctness. Validate internal consistency and do not infer an unmet requirement from missing evidence. A high score cannot override a confirmed unmet hard requirement and is neither a probability nor the sole notification gate.

Deterministic code validates contracts and references, enforces known deadlines and confirmed objective constraints, compares reliable numeric values, calculates unions of reliable intervals, applies source/privacy policies and controls workflow, exact identity, retries, deduplication and notifications. Uncertain dates, rates, durations and contract forms stay unknown. A confirmed expired deadline can avoid a paid call. Semantic duplicate suggestions do not establish canonical identity. The model cannot execute tools, change policies, access credentials, send notifications or submit applications.

Application disposition remains `promising` when material hard requirements are sufficiently supported, `potentially_promising` when otherwise aligned but information or evidence is incomplete, and `unsuitable` on a confirmed disqualifier. Warm-seat indications are warnings or configurable policy. Preserve source conflicts and uncertainty. Provider failure, contradictory output or unverifiable evidence yields a controlled error or unknown, never fabricated success.

Record input versions or content hashes, relevant preferences, provider/model identifier, prompt/schema versions, timestamps and outcome. Avoid duplicate paid calls for unchanged inputs where appropriate; invalidate when relevant inputs or versions change. Do not log raw CV, vacancy or mailbox content by default. Apply [source policies](source-policies.md) before external processing of Gmail-derived content.
